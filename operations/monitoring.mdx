---
title: "Monitor identity services"
description: "Establish a baseline and detect SSO or provisioning failures early"
---

Monitor Mintie together with your identity provider. Provider-side sign-in and provisioning reports are the primary source of operational data while Mintie's [audit-log dashboard](/security/audit-logs) is still planned.

## Record a healthy baseline

After launch, save the following values in your team runbook:

| Signal | Healthy baseline |
| --- | --- |
| Connection status | `Active` |
| Test sign-in | Succeeds for an assigned standard user |
| SAML or OIDC application | Enabled and assigned to the expected groups |
| SCIM test user | Create, update, and deactivate complete successfully |
| Admin ownership | At least two current administrators |

Never record client secrets, signing keys, SCIM tokens, or API keys in the runbook.

## Weekly check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Inspect the connection">
    Open **Settings → Auth → SSO** and confirm the provider, connection ID,
    status, and last test result are expected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a test sign-in">
    Use a standard test account. A successful admin session alone does not
    prove that application assignments and member claims are correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review provider reports">
    Look for changes in failed sign-ins, unassigned users, SCIM retries, and
    disabled connectors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check recent changes">
    Ask whether anyone changed redirect URIs, SAML metadata, claims, group
    mappings, credentials, or conditional-access policies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signals to investigate

- A test sign-in changes from success to failure.
- Failures affect multiple users or more than one network.
- The IdP reports invalid audience, issuer, redirect URI, or signature.
- Provisioning retries grow or a deactivated user remains active.
- A connection unexpectedly returns to `Awaiting verification` or `Disconnected`.
- Support requests spike immediately after an IdP policy or certificate change.

## When a check fails

1. Record the first failure time and the last known successful test.
2. Determine whether the issue affects one user, one group, or the whole organization.
3. Check your IdP's service status and recent configuration changes.
4. Follow the [SSO troubleshooting](/operations/troubleshoot-sso) or [SCIM troubleshooting](/operations/troubleshoot-scim) runbook.
5. If access is broadly affected, begin the [incident-response runbook](/operations/incident-response).
