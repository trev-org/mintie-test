---
title: "Enterprise access reviews"
description: "Review privileged access, identity assignments, and recovery ownership"
groups: ["enterprise"]
---

Run an access review at least quarterly and after major organizational changes.
The review should cover Mintie administrators, identity-provider assignments,
provisioning scope, service accounts, and recovery access.

## Review scope

| Area | Confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Administrators | Every administrator still requires elevated access |
| IdP assignments | Only approved users and groups can launch Mintie |
| SCIM scope | Provisioning assignments match the intended population |
| Service accounts | Each account has an active owner and documented purpose |
| API keys | Keys are active, attributable, and rotated according to policy |
| Recovery access | At least two current owners can execute the recovery procedure |

## Run the review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Establish the review population">
    Export or record the current Mintie administrators, IdP application
    assignments, SCIM assignments, service accounts, and API-key owners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect owner attestations">
    Ask application and team owners to confirm each assignment. Require a
    business justification for privileged and non-human access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investigate differences">
    Compare Mintie access with your source-of-truth directory. Resolve orphaned
    accounts, unexpected assignments, and users whose role has changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove unnecessary access">
    Revoke access in the authoritative system first, then verify that the
    change reaches Mintie through SSO or provisioning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record approval">
    Store the reviewer, completion date, exceptions, remediation owners, and
    next review date in your approved governance system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Evidence to retain

- Review scope and source systems
- Reviewer and application-owner approvals
- Exceptions with owners and expiration dates
- Removal or remediation timestamps
- Results of one standard-user and one administrator sign-in test

<Warning>
  Do not place passwords, access tokens, private keys, session cookies, or
  complete SAML assertions in review evidence.
</Warning>
