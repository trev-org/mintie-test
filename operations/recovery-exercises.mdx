---
title: "SSO recovery exercises"
description: "Validate emergency access and identity recovery before an outage"
groups: ["enterprise"]
---

Run a scheduled recovery exercise at least twice a year. The goal is to prove
that authorized responders can diagnose an identity outage, preserve
administrator access, choose a safe mitigation, and restore normal sign-in.

## Exercise objectives

- Reach the current incident and identity-provider owners.
- Locate the approved recovery procedure without relying on SSO.
- Confirm that at least two administrators can perform recovery actions.
- Identify the affected identity flow from provider and Mintie signals.
- Validate rollback and post-recovery checks.

<Warning>
  Use a test environment or tabletop simulation whenever possible. Do not
  disable production SSO or revoke production credentials solely to create an
  exercise condition.
</Warning>

## Suggested scenario

Simulate an identity-provider configuration change that causes all new SSO
attempts to fail while existing sessions remain active. Provide responders
with a failure timestamp and a redacted provider error, but do not reveal the
cause.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Declare the exercise">
    Name the incident lead, Mintie owner, IdP owner, communications owner, and
    observer. Record the start time and expected duration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect recovery access">
    Confirm that responders would keep active administrator sessions open and
    avoid destructive changes until the scope is understood.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Diagnose the failure">
    Compare the last successful sign-in with recent provider changes. Identify
    the most likely audience, issuer, certificate, claim, or assignment issue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select and rehearse mitigation">
    Walk through rollback of the simulated change and the approval required for
    any emergency-access procedure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate recovery">
    Test administrator and standard-user sign-in. If provisioning is in scope,
    validate one complete user lifecycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pass criteria

The exercise passes when responders can complete the procedure without shared
credentials, identify the correct rollback, validate both privileged and
standard access, and assign owners and due dates to every discovered gap.

Afterward, update the [incident-response runbook](/operations/incident-response)
and recovery contacts while the findings are current.
