Explore connected vertices with a queue or stack while preventing duplicate work.

## When to use

- Find connected components.
- Explore grids.
- Determine reachability.

## Steps

1. Choose a starting vertex.
2. Mark vertices before revisiting them.
3. Add each unvisited neighbor to the frontier.

## Complexity

| Measure | Typical cost |
| --- | --- |
| Time | `O(vertices + edges)` |
| Space | `O(vertices)` |
